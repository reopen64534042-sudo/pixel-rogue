Use case: stylized-concept / proposed seed frame for a new missile weapon in Pixel Rogue.
Create ONE isolated right-facing heavy guided missile in flight, on a genuinely transparent background. This is the FIRST visual seed, not a sprite animation sheet and not a UI screenshot.
View: orthographic top-down with a tiny amount of visible upper hull, suitable for a 2D pixel roguelike. Long axis exactly horizontal, nose points RIGHT, thrust behind to the LEFT. Keep everything including exhaust inside the canvas with generous transparent padding.
Art direction: exquisite controlled pixel clusters, deliberate stepped hard edges, a strong compact readable silhouette. A small bone-white tapered armoured hull with dark navy-purple underside, an angular gold collar, swept dark cobalt stabilizer fins, one very small cyan seeker lens in the nose. The nose is metallic with a tiny sensor, never a large glowing energy ball. Three visibly distinct material groups: pale armour, dark blue fins, small warm brass attachments. No text, insignia, faces or logos.
Rear engine: a compact white-hot throat, golden inner flame, forked orange exhaust tapering to just a few deep orange embers. The flame should make a sharp asymmetrical aerodynamic shape, not a long fuzzy cone. Keep the metal missile completely readable. No detached smoke clouds in this seed.
Scale intent: final body silhouette is roughly 16 by 7 game pixels, with thrust 9 by 5 game pixels; render this as a crisp enlarged pixel study so these clusters and material breaks remain readable when reduced. Avoid tiny surface greebles. Use a deliberate sparse logical grid, square pixel blocks; no soft anti-aliasing, painterly shading, 3D render, gradients, lens flare, huge bloom, streak background, scenery, ground shadow, labels, checkerboard or multiple objects.
Restricted palette families from the game: #1a0a2a #22223a #3a1a0a #e8e8f0 #ffffff #3d6de0 #1e3a8a #f4a940 #ffd24a #ffe14a #ff8c3a #c2410c #57e6ff. Transparent alpha outside the subject. Place the single missile across the middle of a landscape canvas. It should feel weighty, precise and hand-crafted, compatible with both single heavy launches and later small coordinated salvos.
